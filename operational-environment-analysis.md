# Operational Environment Analysis

The bank operates in a coastal area with strict financial regulations and manages sensitive financial data for both individual and commercial customers. Because many employees and systems handle customer information, risks such as business email compromise, financial record leaks, and supply chain attacks could potentially affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the bank’s assets.

Environmental risks such as hurricanes could also disrupt operations and impact supply chain availability. These conditions increase the need for strong cybersecurity controls and continuous risk management practices.

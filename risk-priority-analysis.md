# Risk Priority Analysis

| Risk                      | Likelihood | Severity | Priority Score |
| ------------------------- | ---------- | -------- | -------------- |
| Business Email Compromise | 3          | 3        | 9              |
| Compromised User Database | 2          | 3        | 6              |
| Financial Records Leak    | 2          | 3        | 6              |
| Theft                     | 1          | 2        | 2              |
| Supply Chain Attack       | 2          | 2        | 4              |

## Risk Formula

```text
Likelihood × Severity = Priority Score
```

## Priority Interpretation

* 1-3 = Low priority
* 4-6 = Moderate priority
* 7-9 = High priority

# Summary

In this project, cybersecurity risks affecting a commercial bank were analyzed using a risk register methodology. Risks were evaluated based on likelihood and severity to determine overall priority scores.

The assessment identified business email compromise and financial data exposure as the highest-priority risks due to their potential financial, operational, and regulatory impact. This project demonstrates practical cybersecurity risk analysis and governance skills used in security operations and risk management environments.

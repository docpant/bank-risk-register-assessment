# Risk Severity Analysis

| Risk | Severity Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Business Email Compromise | 3 | Could lead to financial fraud and unauthorized access |
| Compromised User Database | 3 | Exposure of customer data may cause regulatory penalties and reputational damage |
| Financial Records Leak | 3 | Financial data exposure could severely impact customers and business operations |
| Theft | 2 | Physical theft may disrupt operations but has lower overall impact |
| Supply Chain Attack | 2 | Could interrupt banking operations and availability of services |

## Scoring Scale

- 1 = Low severity
- 2 = Moderate severity
- 3 = High severity
